## History in ewe browser
keys: browser history, browsing history, my history, visited sites, site i visited, page i visited, clear history, delete history, clear browsing data
Ctrl+H shows the history in ewe browser's sidebar. With an ewe browser that has its command line (newer than 0.1.0-beta), you can ask me: "find tax in my history" or "that site about sheep", and a click opens the page. Coming in the browser: a full history page with search, delete by site and Clear browsing data.

## Tabs in ewe browser
keys: browser tabs, my tabs, open tabs, switch tab, go to tab, youtube tab, which tabs
Ask me "show my tabs" or "go to the YouTube tab" (with an ewe browser newer than 0.1.0-beta, which has its command line). In the browser, the command palette finds tabs with `t:`.

## Sync in ewe browser
keys: browser sync, sync browser, sync bookmarks, sync history, tabs from other computers, other computer tabs, browser on another computer
ewe browser syncs bookmarks, history and settings through your ewe account's Nextcloud. Coming: deleting history syncs too, the open tabs of your other computers show in the browser, and the sync state shows in ewe-sync, which owns all syncing.

## Safe in ewe browser
keys: safe in browser, safe in the browser, browser passwords, fill in browser, autofill browser, browser login, password in browser, password in the browser, login in the browser, login suggestions
Today ewe browser fills a login through the Passwords plugin: Ctrl+Shift+L in the browser, or Super+P anywhere, with Safe as the password manager. Coming: Safe built in, like 1Password: your logins appear under the login field when you click it, and a click fills it; passkeys too. Nothing fills on its own. Or ask me "log me in to GitHub": I open the site and you fill it there.
